Schedule

Turn the board off at night, dim it in the evening or play a different playlist at certain times. A rule that runs past midnight belongs to the day it starts. Pressing Power on the control page during quiet hours turns the board on until they end.

Playback

Seconds per scene

Data stays fresh for (minutes)

How long pushed data (POST /api/data/<key>) is shown before a scene treats it as stale. Empty uses the plugin's default.

On screen

What's drawn over every scene: how one scene turns into the next, a clock or reading in a corner of GIFs and art, and status dots.

Notifications

Write one and send it now, set how notifications look, and add the ones the board raises by itself: reminders at a time of day, and watches that fire when a reading or the weather changes.

Plugins

Each plugin's options, its scenes and the data it has received. The switch at the right turns it on or off.

Holidays and seasons

Each season and holiday has a scene and a GIF folder; they play while it's on.

Untick one to skip it: no scene and no GIFs from its folder.

Your holidays

Add Diwali, Lunar New Year, an anniversary, a team's game days. Upload GIFs to one on the GIFs page and they play while it's on. Dates go one range per line: 11-08 to 11-12 every year, or 2027-02-06 to 2027-02-10 for that year only. A single date is one day.

Appearance

Buttons, links and highlights. Text on it turns dark or light to stay readable.

Display hardware

These take effect after a restart.

Access

The board only answers to its IP addresses and its own names (). Add any other name you open it by, like a reverse proxy's. This blocks DNS rebinding attacks.

Backup and restore

Download your settings (playlist, schedule, timings, plugin settings, GIF categories) as a file. Secrets like API keys and the token stay on the board and are kept when you restore.
