# Design decisions

Joe's verdicts on PixelPane. They stand until he changes them. Add each new one the moment he gives it, one dated line.

## Web UI

- 2026-11-18: No access token for changes in the web UI. It runs on Joe's home network only; he asked three times to remove it and hates it. Never bring it back.
- 2026-11-18: The look is option B, "lit pixels". The old terminal-window look is gone.

## GIFs

- 2026-11-18: New GIFs are highly animated, smooth, colorful and dynamic, on black backgrounds.
- 2026-11-18: GIF ideas are shown as an HTML mockup page, opened for Joe, never as a text list.
- 2026-11-18: Cleanups: never add black spots inside an image when blackening a background; scan every cleaned GIF for them.
- 2026-11-18: These originals beat their cleanups and stay as they were (names as Joe dictated them; the GIFs live on the device, not in this repo): abstract chrome torus, grayscale wave swirl, color spiral, cyan block slide, neon infinity loop, neon scribble loops, rainbow arc spin, rainbow hand fam, rainbow infinity ribbon, rainbow ring arc, rainbow ring spiral; space teal planet clouds; retrosonic sprint green.
- 2026-11-18: Rejected cleanups: the Mario one and every spinning-coin one. The apple is fine. Effects added on top of the retro red and green gems didn't help.
- 2026-11-18: Movie GIFs use real frames from the film, brought to life with motion. Never a still that only pulses in and out of focus.
- 2026-11-18: Film picks: offer a list of 20 films, recent ones included, and let Joe choose.
